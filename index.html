Zoolomino

Animal Block Puzzle
Drop animal blocks onto the wooden tray and fill a row or column. When a line completes, every animal caught in it leaps free. Blocks never rotate, so each piece is a real decision.

Features

  • Every animal has its own shape, and every rescue is recorded in your collection
  • Combos multiply your score
  • No banners, no interstitials — rewarded ads only, and only if you choose
  • Plays offline · No account · 40 languages
